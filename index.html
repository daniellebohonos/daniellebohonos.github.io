I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science at the University of Toronto, where I study comparative politics and Canadian government. My research examines how social identities shape citizens' experiences with the state — how people interact with public institutions in their daily lives, and how those encounters condition trust in government.

My dissertation, The Politics of Distrust: How Social Identities Condition Public Service Experiences, is advised by Dr. Daniel Rubenson. Before Toronto I completed an MA in Political Science at McGill University, with a thesis on elite populist rhetoric during the 2022 Freedom Convoy.

I am a Doctoral Fellow at the Policy, Elections, and Representation Lab and the Media Ecosystem Observatory, and my research is supported by a Canada Graduate Scholarship to Honour Nelson Mandela (SSHRC).
